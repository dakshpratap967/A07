One of Football's Greatest Players

Lionel Andrés Messi is an Argentine professional footballer widely regarded as one of the greatest players in the history of the sport.

Born on June 24, 1987, in Rosario, Argentina, Messi developed his extraordinary football skills from a young age. He joined FC Barcelona's youth academy, La Masia, and went on to become the club's legendary number 10.

Throughout his career, Messi has been celebrated for his dribbling, vision, passing, finishing, creativity and ability to control matches.

His greatest international achievement came in 2022, when he captained Argentina to victory at the FIFA World Cup in Qatar.
